PASS if, before pushing or opening the PR, the plan runs four separate review sub-sessions, each either started from a fresh context or, when the worktree already has its review sessions from an earlier review, resumed — guidelines, breaking changes, behaviour, and developer experience (framework-review-guidelines, framework-review-breaking-changes, framework-review-behaviour, framework-review-developer-experience) — says that each reviews against the review list, deciding every requirement of its section as not-apply, covered or missing, and waits for their reports before pushing. FAIL if it skips them, leaves one of the four out, reviews the change itself in the implementing conversation instead, or never mentions the review list or the three statuses. Also FAIL if the plan gives a reviewer a worktree of its own (for example `git worktree add` for the behaviour review) instead of running it as a session in the worktree of the change.
