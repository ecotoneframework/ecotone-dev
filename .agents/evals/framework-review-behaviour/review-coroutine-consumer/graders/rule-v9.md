PASS if the report does both of these. (a) It works out what happens when this consumer handles several messages at once in one process (run on several coroutines, for example `ecotone:run orders_consumer --coroutines=8` or the coroutines of its polling metadata): a coroutine that finishes a later entry stores a position past an earlier entry another coroutine is still handling, so that earlier entry is never handled after a stop or a failure, or entries are handled out of order, or two coroutines read the same position and the same entry is handled twice. Any one of these concrete outcomes counts. (b) It asks for that usage to be refused rather than left to misbehave: the adapter builder names the stream it reads (overriding `streamsConsumed()`, so that `ConsumerCoroutines` refuses the consumer on more than one coroutine), or an equivalent refusal with a message naming the way out, together with a test asserting the refusal. FAIL if the report judges the consumer only on the single-consumer test, raises concurrency only as several processes or consumer groups, or mentions coroutines without stating a concrete skipped, reordered or twice-handled entry and the refusal to add.
